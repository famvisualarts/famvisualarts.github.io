The “Sik” series was born from my wish to capture a period of my life marked by countless health problems, from the most serious to the smallest, and an abundance of medication. Medical leaflets became the medium through which I express my state of mind during those very difficult times. Usually devoid of emotion, they turn into a way of showing the highs and lows and the inner turmoil I went through.

Through this series I share my inner journey, hoping it will resonate with those who have also lived through hard times. Art can be a cathartic way to transcend the darkest moments of our lives and offer a glimmer of hope.
