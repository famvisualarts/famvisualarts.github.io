FAM is a French visual artist, born in 1987, who lives and works near Paris.

She earned a master’s degree in computer engineering at Panthéon-Sorbonne University and spent two years on a doctorate there. Beyond code and algorithms, a deeper passion was at work: she chose to devote herself entirely to art, leaving certainties behind.

Growing up in an exclusively female environment gave her an intimate knowledge of how femininity is portrayed. Her narrative paintings, centred on this theme, are imaginary and often provocative constructions in which parts of her own life become surreal or hyperrealist pastiches.

FAM removes any ambiguity about who the subject is, so that the viewer’s attention moves to the way it is expressed. Backgrounds and objects echo the body language of the women she paints, shown with restrained emotion, and invite the viewer to empathise with their inner world.

In recent years she has taken part in group exhibitions in France and abroad, including the Salon des artistes locaux at La Graineterie art centre in Houilles (49th, 50th and 51st editions, 2022–2024), Décembre à Montreuil (2023), A Space Arts in Southampton (UK, 2023), Art Boom NFT at Focus Art Fair, Carrousel du Louvre, Paris (2022), Ghost Gallery in Seattle (US, 2022), Anonyme Zeichner at Galerie im Körnerpark, Berlin (2022), and Higher Art Gallery, Michigan (US, 2024). She was nominated for the Prix de Novembre à Vitry, 50th edition (2019).
