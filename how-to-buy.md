<p class="faq-intro">Every work in the shop is a one-of-a-kind original. You buy it directly from the artist, with no middleman.</p>

## Buying online

1. Choose a work in the [shop]({{ '/shop/' | relative_url }}) and open its page.
2. Click **Buy this original**.
3. Enter your delivery address, pick the shipping rate for your country, then pay.
4. You get a receipt by email, and I contact you to confirm the shipment.

A work can only be sold once: as soon as it is paid for, it is no longer available.

## Payment

Card payment is handled by Stripe on a secure page. I never see your card details. Prices are in euros, all taxes included (VAT not applicable, article 293 B of the French tax code).

## Shipping

Every work is shipped within **48 hours** (working days), packed by hand, tracked and insured.

| Format | France | Europe | Rest of the world |
|---|---|---|---|
| Work on paper | €15 | €30 | €60 |
| Painting up to 55 cm | €25 | €40 | €95 |
| Large format (89 cm and over) | €70 | €120 | on quote |

For a large format outside Europe, [write to me]({{ '/contact/' | relative_url }}) for a quote.

## Certificate of authenticity

Every original is signed and comes with a signed certificate of authenticity.

## See it at home

On each work for sale, a to-scale view shows the work above a 160 cm sideboard, so you can judge its real size. For more photos or hanging advice, [write to me]({{ '/contact/' | relative_url }}).

## Changing your mind

You have 14 days after delivery to withdraw, with no reason needed. Details are on the [Shipping and returns]({{ '/legal/shipping/' | relative_url }}) page.

## A question, or a commission?

Write to me from the [Contact]({{ '/contact/' | relative_url }}) page or at [famartwork@gmail.com](mailto:famartwork@gmail.com).
