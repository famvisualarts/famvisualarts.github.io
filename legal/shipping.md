## Shipping

Each work is packed by hand with corner protection, tissue paper and reinforced cardboard. Large canvases ship in a crate or double-wall box.

Shipping costs are shown before payment and depend on the destination country. Works travel by tracked, insured parcel and leave within **[to complete: 3 to 7] working days** of the order.

Delivery areas: **[to complete: France, European Union, United Kingdom, United States…]**.

Every original comes with a signed certificate of authenticity.

## Right of withdrawal

You have 14 days from receiving the work to withdraw from the purchase without giving a reason. Write to [famartwork@gmail.com](mailto:famartwork@gmail.com) before the deadline.

Return the work in its original packaging, in good condition, within 14 days of your request. Return shipping is at your expense. The refund, including the original delivery cost, is made within 14 days of receiving the work, using the same payment method.

## Damaged work

If the work arrives damaged, photograph the parcel and the work and write to me within 48 hours: I will handle the claim with the carrier.
